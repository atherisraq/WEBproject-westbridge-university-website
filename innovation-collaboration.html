Start a collaboration

  1. Tell us your challenge

    Send a short brief through the inquiry form, choosing “Research collaboration”.

  2. Meet the right team

    The Research Office matches you with a center and faculty lead.

  3. Agree scope and terms

    We draft a simple agreement covering scope, timelines, funding and intellectual property.

  4. Work together

    Students and researchers deliver milestones, with regular check-ins and a final showcase.
