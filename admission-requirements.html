Admission requirements

Everything you need to be considered for an undergraduate or graduate place at Westbridge.

Undergraduate entry

  • Completion of higher secondary education (HSC, A-Levels, IB Diploma, or recognised equivalent) with a minimum overall result of GPA 3.5 / 5.0 or equivalent.
  • Subject prerequisites for your chosen program (for example Mathematics and Physics for Engineering; Mathematics for Business and Economics).
  • Evidence of English proficiency: IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 80, or equivalent. Applicants from English-medium schools may request a waiver.
  • A personal statement of up to 500 words and one academic reference.

Graduate entry

  • A bachelor’s degree with a minimum CGPA of 3.0 / 4.0 (or second-class honours) in a relevant discipline.
  • Two academic or professional references.
  • Statement of purpose; PhD applicants also submit a two-page research proposal.
  • MBA applicants need two years of professional experience; other master’s programs do not.

Document checklist

  • Certified copies of academic transcripts and certificates
  • Copy of passport or national ID
  • Passport-size photograph (digital, white background)
  • English proficiency evidence, where applicable
  • Reference letters sent directly by your referees

Documents that are not in English or Bangla must be accompanied by a certified translation.
