Welcome to Westbridge

A university built on the idea that learning connects people.

Our name says it all. Westbridge is a place where disciplines meet, where students from different countries study side by side, and where research turns into real benefit for society.

From your first lecture to your final project, you will be taught by faculty who are active researchers and practitioners, supported by advisors who know your name, and challenged to think beyond the classroom.
