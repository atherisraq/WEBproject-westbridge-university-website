Who we are

Westbridge International University is a private, non-profit university offering undergraduate, graduate and doctoral programs across six faculties. Our students come from more than 60 countries, learning from faculty who are active researchers and practitioners.

Our story

  1. 1992

    A university is founded

    Westbridge opens with 300 students and two faculties on a single campus.

  2. 2003

    First graduate programs

    Master’s degrees launch in engineering and business.

  3. 2011

    Research institutes established

    The first interdisciplinary research centers are created.

  4. 2018

    International campus expansion

    International House and the Innovation Hub open.

  5. 2024

    Doctoral programs expand

    New PhD programs in natural and social sciences begin.

  6. 2026

    A new chapter

    Record international enrolment and new exchange partnerships.
